The Pearl Harvesting Grounds

A Special Fishing Village

Mingzhu originates from an idyllic fishing village blessed with bountiful pearl harvesting grounds. The waters yield the most radiant, finest pearls in the world, drawing curious merchants and travelers from afar. Mingzhu’s cherished dream is to cultivate a breathtaking pearl of unmatched purity to pass down as a treasured family heirloom.
