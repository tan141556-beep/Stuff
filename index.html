Mingzhu: The Pearl Picker

Appearance & Identity

Her name is Mingzhu. She has lustrous black hair neatly woven into thick braids, warm light brown eyes, and stands around 5'3" tall. Dressed in simple, practical linen garments styled like a traditional fisher's attire, she is ready to face ocean waves at any moment.
